Complete this **before** architecture design begins (see [Phase 1](../06-lifecycle/phase-1-define-baseline.md)). Sign-off required from both the business owner and FinOps lead.

---

**Workload name:**
_(e.g., "Tier-1 customer support agent — billing inquiries")_

**Outcome definition:**
_What specifically counts as "successful"? Be precise enough that two people would agree, independently, whether a given task succeeded. See [Value Progression](../03-value-economics/value-progression.md)._

**Value per outcome:**
_Monetary or proxy value, and the source of that figure. See [Value Calculation](../03-value-economics/value-calculation.md) and [benchmarking guidance](../03-value-economics/value-per-outcome-benchmarks.md)._

**Baseline cost (pre-AI):**
_Cost of achieving this outcome today, without AI — needed to calculate Net AI Value later._

**Expected volume:**
_Outcomes per month/quarter._

**Value Leakage risks:**
_Name the specific ways realized value could be overstated for this workload. See [Common Value Leakage Sources](../03-value-economics/value-leakage.md#common-sources)._

**How Value Leakage will be measured:**
_e.g., "Reopened-ticket rate within 7 days, tracked in the ticketing system."_

**Escalation tolerance:**
_What Value Density or Net AI Value threshold, sustained over what period, triggers the [escalation review](../07-governance/escalation-and-scale-retire.md)?_
