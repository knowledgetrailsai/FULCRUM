# Phase 6 — Optimize & Scale

`Home › 06-lifecycle › Phase 6`

[← Previous: Phase 5, Operate & Attribute](phase-5-operate-attribute.md) · [Contents](../README.md) · [Next: Decision Rights →](../07-governance/decision-rights.md)

**Objective:** Use accumulated cost and value data to decide what scales, what gets re-architected, and what gets retired.

## Activities

1. **Rank workflows by Value Density**, not by volume or visibility. See [Cost & Value Density](../04-measurement-and-observability/cost-value-density.md).
2. **Reuse validated cost/value patterns**: routing rules, evaluation designs, context strategies — across new workloads.
3. **Retire or re-architect workflows with sustained low Value Density**, following the [governance escalation path](../07-governance/escalation-and-scale-retire.md).
4. Feed learnings back into [Phase 1](phase-1-define-baseline.md) for new or revised workloads.

## Exit Criteria

None — this phase feeds back into Phase 1 for new or revised workloads, closing the lifecycle loop (see [Lifecycle Overview](lifecycle-overview.md#the-loop-not-the-line)).

## Owner

Platform + business leadership, jointly.

## Common Pitfalls

- Scaling by replicating individual pilots one at a time, instead of reusing validated platform components and governance controls across workloads
- Allowing sustained negative Net AI Value to persist because the workflow is politically visible or was an early flagship pilot
- Treating this phase as a one-time portfolio review rather than the standing quarterly cadence in [07-governance/review-gates.md](../07-governance/review-gates.md)
