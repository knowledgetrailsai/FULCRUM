## Trigger

Sustained **negative Net AI Value** on a workflow triggers mandatory review at [Phase 6 — Optimize & Scale](../06-lifecycle/phase-6-optimize-scale.md).

## Outcomes of That Review

1. **Re-architect**: apply the [Six Optimization Levers](../05-architecture-and-design/optimization-levers.md) to close the gap
2. **Re-scope**: narrow the workload to the subset where Value Density is positive
3. **Retire**: decommission the workflow if neither option closes the gap within an agreed timeframe

## What Counts as "Sustained"

A single bad week is noise; a persistent trend across the [weekly/monthly review cadence](review-gates.md#review-cadence) is signal. Define the specific threshold and lookback window per workflow at [Phase 1](../06-lifecycle/phase-1-define-baseline.md), as part of the Outcome & Value Definition Sheet's tolerance settings.
