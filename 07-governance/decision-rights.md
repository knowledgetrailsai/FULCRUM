| Decision | Owner |
|---|---|
| Outcome and value definition | Business/product owner |
| Cost architecture (routing, context design, evaluation depth) | Engineering/architecture lead |
| Ongoing attribution and reporting | FinOps lead |
| Scale, re-architect, or retire | Platform + business leadership, jointly |

Full role detail and rationale for this split: [01-foundations/stakeholder-roles.md](../01-foundations/stakeholder-roles.md).

## Why Decision Rights Are Split, Not Centralized

Centralizing all economic decisions in one role creates a conflict of interest — the team building the system is rarely the right team to also set the value case that justifies it. Splitting ownership forces both ledgers to be independently defensible.
